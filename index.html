What's inside

More than one flap

Built with a lot of small details that add up to a game you'll actually keep opening.

Custom bird photo

Pick any photo from your gallery and it becomes your bird. Yes, that means your face can flap through pipes.

Full look control

Six backgrounds and six pipe colors to mix and match, so your run never looks like anyone else's.

Same-WiFi 2P battle

Host a match or join by IP. Same pipes for both players — whoever survives longer wins, bragging rights included.

Auto Play mode

Let the AI take the wheel and watch it fly the gaps perfectly. Great for background chill or seeing how it's done.

Achievements

Getting Warm, Flap Master, and more — unlockable milestones that track your progress in real modes.

Golden pipes

Spot a golden pipe and fly it clean for a +5 score bonus. Small risk, bigger flex on the leaderboard.

Pick your pain

Seven modes, one bird

Every difficulty is a different personality. Auto and Zen scores don't count toward BEST — those are just for fun.

Easy — Chill pipes

Wide gaps, low stress. Good for warming up your thumb.

Medium — Classic

The original balance. This is where BEST scores are usually made.

Hard — Tiny gaps

Precision only. One wrong tap and you're restarting.

Auto Play — AI flies

Sit back and watch the bird thread every gap by itself.

Zen — No death, relax

Pipes are there, but you can't lose. Pure flapping meditation.

Insane — Moving pipes

The pipes shift as you approach. Reflexes only, no memorizing.

Daily Challenge

A fresh, fixed layout every day — same seed for everyone playing.
